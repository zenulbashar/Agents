# About prompt2eat

prompt2eat lets restaurant and venue owners publish an AI-powered menu and
ordering experience for their diners. Venue owners sign up, configure their
venue and menu, and share a link or QR code with diners.

Support departments:
- **tech** — product issues, bugs, login problems, integrations
- **sales** — plans, upgrades, and pre-sales questions
- **billing** — invoices, payments, and subscription changes
