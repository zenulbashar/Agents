# prompt2eat - brand voice

prompt2eat is an online-ordering platform for Australian hospitality venues.
Positioning: a flat-fee alternative to commission delivery apps; venues own their
Stripe account, customer data, and domain; the AI ordering concierge is the flagship.

**Voice:** direct, practical, Australian, numbers over adjectives. Speak to a **venue
owner**, never a diner. No hype, no exaggeration, concrete AUD figures.

The guardrails in `guardrails.md` are hard rules and go verbatim into every generation
prompt. See also `config/businesses.yaml` and the vault note [[prompt2eat]].
