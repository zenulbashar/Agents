# Outreach rules (Spam Act 2003, ACMA)

Every commercial email carries **legal name + ABN + postal address + a functional
unsubscribe** honoured within 5 business days (no login required).

- **Newsletter list:** express consent only - people who signed up. Never import scraped addresses.
- **Cold-ish B2B outreach:** the only lawful basis is the Act's *inferred consent via
  conspicuous publication* - and it is conditional: the address is **publicly published by
  the business**, carries no "no marketing" note, and your message is **directly relevant to
  the recipient's role**. An online-ordering pitch to a cafe's published contact plausibly
  qualifies; blasting `info@` generically does not. Publication alone is NOT consent (ACMA 2024).
- Hence the design: research each venue **individually**, draft a **personalised, relevant**
  email, **you approve each row**, n8n sends **<=10/day**, every send carries ABN + unsubscribe,
  opt-outs are honoured immediately and logged.
- The moment anyone replies with interest, move them to the express-consent list.

Not legal advice - the compliance-shaped design. If outreach becomes a major channel, spend an hour with a lawyer.
