# prompt2eat

#business/prompt2eat

Online-ordering platform for Australian hospitality venues. Flat-fee alternative to
commission delivery apps; venues own their Stripe account, customer data, and domain.
The **AI ordering concierge** (type what you feel like -> cart fills) is the flagship.

- **Priority:** primary - market prompt2eat accounts first.
- **Pricing (locked, never invent discounts):** Pro AUD $79/mo; Scale AUD $199/mo;
  1-month free trial at Scale; annual -20%.
- **Channels & caps:** see `config/businesses.yaml` and `marketing/`. X 2/day link-free
  + 1 link/week; IG 1/day; TikTok 2/week (draft mode); newsletter weekly; outreach <=10/weekday.
- **Guardrails:** `marketing/content/guardrails.md` (verbatim in every generation prompt).
- **Rule:** never invent testimonials/venues/customer counts without a row in approved-proof.
